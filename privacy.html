Privacy policy

This policy explains the Flight 217 website and the game’s online features. The controller is Bahar Akca Ucar, trading under the brand bhrkcgame. Contact: flight217official@gmail.com. Availability of online features depends on the build and platform.

Information used and why

ActivityInformation and purpose
Platform sign-in and friendsSteam/Epic account identifiers, display names, authentication tickets and friend identifiers are used to authenticate players, find friends and enable invitations. Steam sign-in may also authenticate through Epic Online Services. Never send us your password or authentication ticket.
Online sessionsEpic Online Services lobby identifiers, Unity Authentication player identifiers, room codes, join information and player names support matchmaking, invitations, reconnects and multiplayer. Unity Multiplayer/Relay carries session traffic. Providers may process IP addresses, device and connection logs to operate and protect their services.
GameplayNames, movement, health, actions and other session state are shared with the host and participating players to synchronize the game.
Text and voiceChat messages and microphone audio are transmitted to other players in the session. The current multiplayer implementation enables team radio by default during connected gameplay; use the radio control and device microphone permissions to control participation. The game does not implement a persistent chat archive or audio recording service. Other participants may record what they receive.
Local settings and diagnosticsPreferences, display name and reconnect information are stored on your device. Local game/engine logs may contain diagnostic and session information. Service providers may maintain operational diagnostics under their own policies.
SupportYour email address, message and any information you choose to send are used to respond to requests and resolve issues. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.
WebsiteThis static website uses no developer-added analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms or cookies. GitHub Pages processes visitor IP addresses and technical logs for hosting and security. Following an external link or emailing us uses the relevant provider’s service.

Providers and recipients

Depending on your platform and features, data is handled by Valve/Steam, Epic Games/Epic Online Services and Unity’s authentication and multiplayer services. Website hosting is provided by GitHub Pages; support email uses Google Gmail. Session data and communications are shared with the host and other participating players. Store purchases and payment details are handled by the store; we do not collect your payment card details through this website.

Provider information: Steam privacy, Epic privacy, Unity privacy, GitHub privacy, Google privacy.

Legal grounds and international processing

Where applicable, we process information needed to provide requested game and support services, protect sessions and troubleshoot faults, comply with legal duties, and establish or defend legal claims. Optional microphone use depends on your choice and device permission; device permission alone is not a general consent to all processing. Turkish legal grounds and rights are described in the KVKK notice.

These global providers may process information outside your country, including outside Türkiye or the EEA. International transfers must satisfy applicable legal requirements. This policy does not by itself constitute consent or a contractual transfer safeguard. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your request or service.

Retention

Voice packets, the short on-screen chat history and session state are used as temporary gameplay buffers rather than a developer-operated archive. Reconnect state is held for session operation. Local settings remain on your device until you reset or remove them. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request, handle related disputes or meet legal obligations. Providers have their own technical-log and account-retention rules; deleting local game data does not delete platform accounts.

Your choices and rights

You can choose solo play, control microphone participation and use platform privacy settings. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of your information, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, and complain to a competent authority. Email the controller with your request and the platform/player identifier needed to locate it. We may request proportionate identity verification; do not email passwords or identity documents unless a secure, necessary verification method is agreed. Some account records must be managed with the relevant platform provider.

Children and updates

Follow the game’s age rating and the account rules of your platform. The website does not request children’s information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal data can contact us. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with a new update date.
